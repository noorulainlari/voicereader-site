How do I transcribe a WhatsApp voice message?

Press and hold the voice message, tap Forward, tap the Share icon, then choose Voice Reader.

I don't see Voice Reader in the share sheet

Scroll the row of apps to the end, tap More, then Edit, and add Voice Reader to your Favorites.

The text is wrong or empty

Make sure the correct audio language is selected. You can transcribe again in another language from the ••• menu of any transcription.

Restore my subscription

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Manage or cancel your subscription in iPhone Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.
